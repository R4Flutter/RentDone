# Privacy Policy for RentDone

Last updated: April 5, 2026

RentDone Technologies ("RentDone", "we", "our", or "us") operates the RentDone mobile application and related services (the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect your information when you use our Services.

This policy is designed to support Google Play policy requirements and to provide transparency for users in India and globally, including users in regions where GDPR may apply.

Website: https://rentdone.app  
Contact: support@rentdone.app

## 1. Introduction

By using RentDone, you agree to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy, subject to applicable law.

If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information collected automatically, and information from trusted third-party service providers.

### 2.1 Personal Data

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Profile photo

### 2.2 Authentication Data

- Firebase user ID (Firebase UID)
- Login provider (for example, email/password, Google Sign-In)
- Session and authentication tokens

### 2.3 Financial and Transaction Data

- Payment transaction details (for example, amount, status, transaction references)
- Subscription status and plan details

Important: card, bank, and payment instrument details are processed by payment processors and are not stored by RentDone except for transaction metadata needed for records, reconciliation, fraud prevention, and support.

### 2.4 Device and Technical Data

- Device identifiers (including mobile advertising/device identifiers where permitted)
- Operating system and version
- App version
- Crash diagnostics and logs

### 2.5 Usage Data

- In-app activity
- Feature interactions
- Session duration and engagement metrics

### 2.6 Location Data (Optional)

- Approximate location (when you grant permission)
- Purpose: to show nearby listings and improve recommendations

You can deny or revoke location permission in your device settings; some location-dependent features may then be limited.

### 2.7 Cookies and Similar Tracking Technologies

Mobile apps typically use SDK-based identifiers and similar technologies rather than browser cookies. RentDone and integrated SDKs may use:

- Analytics tracking
- Performance monitoring
- Advertising-related identifiers (for ads and measurement)
